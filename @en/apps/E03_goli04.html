Privacy Policy

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application “GoL Infinity” (displayed as “GoL∞” on the device; the “Application”), provided by Time Bird (the “Service Provider”).

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Game progress, settings, and other gameplay data are stored only on your device. The Service Provider does not receive, access, or store this local game data. You can delete it by clearing the Application’s storage or uninstalling the Application.

Information Collection and Use

The Service Provider does not require you to create an account and does not ask the Application to collect your name, email address, postal address, contacts, precise location, photos, audio, or other directly identifying information.

The Application uses the third-party services described below to display advertisements and manage the one-time purchase that removes advertisements. These services may automatically process limited device, advertising, diagnostic, interaction, and purchase information.

Advertising Through Google AdMob

Before the advertisement-removal purchase is active, the Application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • Your device’s Internet Protocol (IP) address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, and video views
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, crashes, hangs, and energy usage
  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and other applicable identifiers

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and service delivery. Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Where required by applicable law, consent is requested before personalized advertising or other non-essential processing. Available advertising privacy choices may be managed through the Application’s privacy options or the privacy and advertising controls provided by your device and Google.

After the advertisement-removal purchase becomes active, the Application stops requesting and displaying AdMob advertisements. Purchase verification through RevenueCat and Google Play may continue as described below.

Purchases Through RevenueCat and Google Play

The Application uses RevenueCat and Google Play Billing to offer and verify a one-time purchase that removes advertisements. RevenueCat may process:

  • An anonymous Application User ID generated by RevenueCat
  • Device type, operating system, app version, locale, and related technical information
  • Purchase history, Google purchase tokens, product identifiers, transaction status, and entitlement status
  • The date and time the Application or purchase entitlement was last checked

This information is used to process and validate purchases, restore the advertisement-removal entitlement, prevent fraud, provide customer support, and analyze purchase functionality. The Service Provider does not send your name, email address, postal address, or payment-card details to RevenueCat. Payment information is processed by Google Play, and the Service Provider does not receive your complete payment-card information.

Third-Party Services

The Application uses the following third-party services. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Expo is used only during the development and build process. The released Application does not use Expo services to collect or transmit user data.

Sharing and Disclosure

The Service Provider does not sell your personal data. Information is transmitted to Google and RevenueCat only for the advertising, purchase-processing, entitlement, analytics, security, and fraud-prevention purposes described in this Privacy Policy. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to respond to valid legal process, to protect rights or safety, or to investigate fraud or abuse.

International Data Transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Their privacy policies describe their international data-transfer practices and applicable safeguards. Privacy and data-protection laws in those countries may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local game progress and settings remain on your device until you clear the Application’s storage or uninstall the Application. The Service Provider does not keep a server copy of this data.

Google and RevenueCat retain information according to their respective privacy policies and for as long as necessary to provide advertising, purchase verification, entitlement restoration, security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and related services.

Uninstalling the Application stops future collection by the Application but does not automatically delete information already processed by Google or RevenueCat.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent. You may use the privacy controls provided by Google, Android, and the Application where available. Information processed by Google and RevenueCat is also subject to their respective privacy policies and applicable law. Nothing in this Privacy Policy limits rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.

California Privacy Rights

California residents may have rights to know, correct, or delete personal information, to opt out of certain sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising privacy rights. The Service Provider does not sell personal information. Advertising privacy choices may be available through privacy options in the Application and Google or Android advertising controls.

Children

The Application is not intended for children under 16 years of age, or any higher minimum age required by applicable law. The Service Provider does not knowingly ask children under that age to provide personal data. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal data through the Application, contact timebird.development@gmail.com so that appropriate action can be taken.

Security

The Service Provider limits data handling to what is necessary for the purposes described above. Data sent to Google and RevenueCat is protected in transit using encryption. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Where required by law, additional notice or consent will be provided before a material change takes effect.
